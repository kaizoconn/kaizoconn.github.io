About Us

As a full-service renovation firm run by craftsmen, we look for projects that challenge and inspire creative movement. Honest simple spaces. Refreshing colors. Oaken bookcases. Decorative sheds, tea houses, playhouses.

Interior and exterior improvements in wood, stone, mud and paint. Pergolas and pavilions. Gazebos and guesthouses. No project too large or small — no matter the scope, we provide the attention your project demands to shine.

From start to finish, our team of finish and carpentry professionals will help you add that special touch to your renovation project — making it not only a painless process, but a labor of love.
